This register identifies the NIST sources reviewed for version 2.0, their publication status, and how they inform the hub. A source mapping means that a publication informed a planning field or workflow. It does not claim that NIST authored, approved, or validated the artifact.

Authority matters. Final publications provide the current baseline for their applicable scope. Drafts, project pages, candidate evaluations, and forecast dates are clearly labeled and must not be treated as final requirements. Organization-defined controls, risk scores, and yearly targets require local approval.

Download the source register JSON or artifact crosswalk JSON. See the planning guide for transition-date interpretation and review triggers.
