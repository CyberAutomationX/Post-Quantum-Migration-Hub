Recommended use

  1. Download the XLSX and complete the Crypto_Inventory sheet for priority assets.
  2. Review the calculated risk score and P1/P2/P3 priority.
  3. Use the Assessment_Checklist and Vendor_Questions sheets to collect evidence and identify blockers.
  4. Convert P1/P2 items into a migration roadmap and pilot plan.
  5. Publish only redacted output externally; keep sensitive architecture evidence internal.
